# Code of Conduct

kernoid follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
we want a community free of harassment, where people of every background are welcome, disagree respectfully and
look after each other. It applies in all of kernoid's spaces – the repositories, issues, discussions and events –
and where someone represents the project.

Report behaviour that breaks it to the maintainers through
[a private report on GitHub](https://github.com/kernoid/kernoid/security/advisories/new) or, once it exists, the
project's contact address (OPEN). Reports are handled confidentially; the maintainers decide on consequences as the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
