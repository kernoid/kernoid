The kernoid operator runs in {{ .Release.Namespace }} and watches Kernoid resources in {{ .Values.watchNamespace | default "every namespace" }}.

Describe an installation as a Kernoid resource – an example:
  https://github.com/kernoid/kernoid/blob/main/deploy/kubernetes/example.yaml

  kubectl get kernoids --all-namespaces

Helm installs the CustomResourceDefinition once and never upgrades it; after upgrading the chart, apply the new one:
  kubectl apply --server-side -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kernoid/kernoid/v{{ .Chart.AppVersion }}/deploy/kubernetes/crd.json
